SMS / text messaging program

Stay Connected via Text

MCJ Real Estate Services, Inc. sends SMS messages only to customers and clients who request real estate information or services and explicitly opt in to receive texts. Texts are used for real estate inquiries, requested property updates, appointment and showing confirmations, and service notices.

Who receives SMS messages?

People who submit the MCJ contact or SMS opt-in form and check the unchecked SMS consent checkbox. SMS consent is optional and is not required to receive services.

What messages are sent?

Conversational real estate messages about inquiries, requested property updates, appointment confirmations, showing coordination, and service notices. Message frequency varies.

SMS disclosures: Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Mobile opt-in data will not be shared, sold, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
